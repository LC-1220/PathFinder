Terms and Conditions and Consent Form

Please read the following terms and conditions carefully before uploading your report card.

1. Purpose of the System

This website is developed as part of an academic thesis/research project. The system is designed to process students' academic records through Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and use the extracted academic information to generate possible college course recommendations.

The recommendations provided by the system are intended for research and academic guidance purposes only. They do not constitute official academic, career, or admission advice and do not guarantee admission, academic performance, or success in any recommended course.

2. Information to Be Collected

By using this system, you understand that information contained in your uploaded report card may be collected and processed, including but not limited to:

  • Full name
  • Student number or learner identification number
  • Grade level
  • Academic strand or track
  • Subject names and subject codes
  • Grades
  • General average
  • Academic year or semester
  • Other academic information appearing on the uploaded report card

The uploaded report card may be processed by the system to extract relevant information through OCR.

You are advised not to upload documents containing information that is unrelated to the purpose of this research.

3. Use of Your Information

The information collected from your report card will be used for the following purposes:

  • To extract academic information using OCR.
  • To organize and process your academic records.
  • To analyze your grades and academic performance.
  • To generate possible college course recommendations.
  • To evaluate the functionality and performance of the proposed system.
  • To support the analysis and findings of the academic thesis.

Your information will not be intentionally used for advertising, marketing, or unrelated commercial purposes.

4. Automated Processing

The system may use automated processing to analyze your academic records and generate course recommendations based on the information extracted from your report card.

You understand that automated processing may produce inaccurate or incomplete results, particularly if information is incorrectly extracted through OCR. You should review the extracted information before proceeding.

The recommendations generated by the system should not be considered a final decision regarding your choice of college course.

5. Data Privacy

The collection and processing of your personal information will be conducted in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy laws and regulations, including Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

The system will follow the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality in the processing of personal information.

Reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures will be implemented to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

6. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

Your personal information will only be accessed by authorized individuals involved in the development, administration, evaluation, and research activities of this thesis, subject to applicable privacy and research requirements.

If third-party services are used for necessary functions such as hosting, storage, OCR, or other technical services, the processing of personal information will be subject to applicable privacy requirements and appropriate safeguards.

7. Data Retention

Your personal information and uploaded report card will only be retained for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes of the research and in accordance with the approved research protocol and applicable institutional policies.

After the applicable retention period, personal information and uploaded files will be securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymized when appropriate.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable Philippine data privacy laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of your personal information.
  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain forms of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis for processing.
  • Request deletion or blocking where applicable.
  • Exercise data portability where applicable.
  • File a complaint regarding the processing of your personal information when appropriate.

These rights are subject to the limitations and conditions provided by applicable laws and regulations.

10. Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for ensuring that the report card you upload belongs to you and that the information provided is accurate.

OCR technology may incorrectly recognize characters, numbers, subjects, or grades. You should review the information extracted by the system before it is used for course recommendation.

11. Research Use

The information collected through this system may be used for the academic evaluation and documentation of the thesis.

Where research results are presented in reports, presentations, publications, or other academic materials, reasonable measures will be taken to prevent the unnecessary disclosure of personally identifiable information.
